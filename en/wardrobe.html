What we do

No standard fits every room

Standard wardrobe systems come in fixed widths and heights. Most rooms don't match. The result is filler panels that feel like an afterthought, and storage that never quite works.

We start with your room and your storage needs. Every shelf, rail and drawer is planned around what you actually own – and how you want to access it day to day.

Whether it's a walk-in wardrobe, built-in alcove storage or a wardrobe wall that spans an entire room, we build it from scratch. The result fits the space perfectly and is built to last decades.

How we work

From empty space to perfect order

01

Needs and room assessment

We talk about how you use the wardrobe today, what's missing and what you wish you had. We then measure the room carefully – every slope, every recess, every pipe.

02

Design and material selection

We draw a proposal showing shelf layout, door solution and internal organisation. You choose materials, surfaces and fittings. Fixed price before production begins.

03

Production

Every component is cut and assembled in our workshop with CNC precision. Surfaces are treated before delivery to ensure a flawless finish.

04

Installation

We install and adjust everything on-site. All doors are aligned, all drawers run smoothly. We don't leave until it's right.
