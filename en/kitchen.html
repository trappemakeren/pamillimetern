What we do

A kitchen that actually fits the room

Most kitchens are built around modules of fixed widths – 30, 40, 60 and 80 cm. Your room is rarely an exact combination of these. The result: visible filler strips, lost centimetres and solutions that "almost" fit.

We start with blank sheets and your measurements. Every cabinet is produced in exactly the width and height the room demands. No visible gaps. No compromises. The kitchen uses every single centimetre – from wall to wall, from floor to ceiling.

We draw, produce and install everything ourselves. That means one point of contact – and one phone number to call if anything comes up.

How we work

From first conversation to finished kitchen

01

Enquiry and site visit

You tell us about the room, the budget and what you have in mind. We ask questions, take measurements and look at the room with a craftsman's eye – everything we need to know about slanted walls, awkward pipework and possibilities you may not have seen.

02

Design and quotation

We prepare design proposals with 3D drawings and a fixed price quote. You see exactly how the kitchen will look from every angle – and know exactly what you'll pay. No surprises along the way.

03

Production in the workshop

Once the design and quote are approved, production begins. Every cabinet is cut and assembled in our workshop. We use CNC technology for precision and craftsmanship for the details no machine can handle.

04

Installation and handover

We install at your home over 3–7 days. All adjustments are made on-site – we don't stop until every cabinet, door and drawer is exactly right. We then walk through the kitchen with you and give maintenance advice.

Your choices

Everything can be tailored

Fronts

Lacquered MDF in matt, silk-matt or gloss finish. Solid timber in oak, walnut or ash. Veneer with a natural wood surface. We produce flat, simply profiled and richly profiled fronts.

Worktops

Laminate, compact laminate, solid oak, quartz/composite or natural stone. We help you choose the right material for your use and budget.

Interior fittings

Soft-close hinges and drawers as standard. Pull-out shelves, spice racks, integrated waste sorting, cable drawers and internal cabinet lighting.

Colours

We can produce in virtually any RAL or NCS colour. From classic white to deep navy, olive green, anthracite or bespoke mixed colours.

Appliances

We coordinate with your supplier and adapt the kitchen to the appliances you choose – or help with recommendations.

Lighting

Under-cabinet lighting, internal spot lighting and integrated light sources can be planned from the start – not added as an afterthought.

Investment

What does a custom kitchen cost?

A bespoke kitchen is an investment in something you use every single day for decades to come. The price depends on the size of the room, the choice of materials and the complexity of the interior fittings.

A typical kitchen from us falls in the range of NOK 150,000–400,000 including installation, but excluding appliances and natural stone worktop work. We always provide a fixed price quote after a site visit.

The best way to get a sense of the price is to get in touch. We offer a no-obligation first conversation.
