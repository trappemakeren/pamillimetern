Who we are

Skilled hands.
Modern tools.
No compromises.

På Millimetern is a furniture studio with a showroom in Oslo and production in Begna, Valdres. Behind every project stand Auke, Rob, Tore, Ole Ronny and Tor Henry – craftsmen who have chosen their trade with care.

We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern CNC technology at one of Eastern Norway's best-equipped furniture workshops. This gives us a precision that isn't possible by hand alone – and a final result that isn't possible from a warehouse.

We always start with a blank sheet, and don't stop until every detail is exactly right. Not almost. Not good enough. To the millimetre.
