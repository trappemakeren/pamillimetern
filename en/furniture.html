What we do

Craftsmanship for those who don't want the same as everyone else

Mass-produced furniture is designed for the average room, the average taste, the average person. If you've ever walked into a furniture shop and left empty-handed because nothing was quite right – we might be who you're looking for.

We build furniture that fits the specific dimensions of your room, your specific storage needs and your specific aesthetic. A dining table with the exact length and the exact edge profile you want. A bookshelf that fills the alcove beside the fireplace perfectly. A media unit that houses your vinyl collection alongside your equipment.

If you have an idea, a sketch or a photograph of something you love – get in touch. That's how most of our projects begin.

Examples

Examples of furniture projects

Bookshelves and library walls

Built-in bookshelves that frame a fireplace, fill an entire wall or wrap around a corner. With lighting, ladder rails or display niches as desired.

TV units and media walls

Media furniture designed around your equipment – with space for vinyl, gaming, cables and everything you want hidden. Floor-standing or floating.

Sideboards and consoles

Dining room sideboards in solid oak or walnut. Console tables for hallways. Display cabinets with glass fronts. We make what fits your room.

Dining tables

Solid timber tables with live edges, straight-sawn boards or book-matched veneer. In the length, width and height that works for your space and your family.

Office furniture

Desks, meeting tables, reception counters and storage walls for offices, home offices and commercial spaces.

Other solutions

Stair railings, wall panelling, cabin interiors, retail fixtures. If it can be built in wood, we can help. Tell us what you have in mind.
